CONTROLS

A/← and D/→ move · SPACE/↑ jump · X sniff · Z bark · P pause. Touch buttons work throughout play. Gamepad: A jump, B bark, X sniff, Start pause; D-pad plus A rank evidence. P opens a trail map. E toggles previous-attempt paw trails, M toggles original music. B opens or replays the Golden Sock trail after a perfect case and three Nose Stamps. After finishing the campaign, Ranger Trials lets you race your own or an imported recorded ghost and collect medals (1–3 selects stages, Escape returns).

YOUR JOB

Collect paw tokens, clear dangerous gaps, activate invisible scent bridges, sniff the suspicious object near each exit, then rank the three discoveries. Sniff near raised platforms to reveal one hidden Nose Stamp per stage. Repeated frantic sniffing makes Dexter sulk. Collect all three Nose Stamps and solve the case correctly to unlock a secret Golden Sock challenge. Ranger Trials are optional. Open the Ranger Replay Cartridge desk to export/restore your personal-best routes or race an imported visitor. Nothing is saved or uploaded automatically.
